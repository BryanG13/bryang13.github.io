\section{Profile}
Operations Research specialist with more than seven years of experience across academia and industry, designing optimisation models and algorithms for transportation, logistics and healthcare, and turning them into software that is used in practice. Postdoctoral researcher at the University of Antwerp (ANT/OR) and former Lead Operations Research Expert at Triptomatic, where I owned the optimisation workstream of a production dispatching system from research to deployment. My work combines exact methods, column generation, metaheuristics and real-time optimisation, with five peer-reviewed journal papers, a new metaheuristic framework (Parameter Space Search), and consulting projects for Atlas Copco, De Lijn and the Belgian police.\\
