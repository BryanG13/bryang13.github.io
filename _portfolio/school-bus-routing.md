Not every school journey is a school journey. Transporting students with special needs means the vehicle capacity is constrained by more than headcount: wheelchairs and mobility aids occupy space, individual students have to be picked up at their own address, and the round trip has to fit school start and end times without waiting hours on the kerb.

This project built and solved that capacitated school bus routing problem for the municipalities of **Diest** and **Gavere**, using real De Lijn data — actual travel-time matrices and actual demand — over fleets of up to 18 vehicles. Working from real data rather than synthetic instances made the modelling trade-offs visible in a way benchmark data never does: which constraint actually binds, and which assumptions quietly drive the objective value.
