Invited to present at the KU Leuven CIB (Centre for Industrial Management / Traffic & Infrastructure) internal research seminar on multiple occasions (2019, 2021, and 2022). The talk focused on semi-flexible bus services, discussing models and optimization approaches for demand-responsive transit systems with both mandatory and optional stops.
